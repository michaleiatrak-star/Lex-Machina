## Goal

Remove transport decisions from the model.

Before G18 the verification tool accepted:
- legal claim;
- kind;
- official URL;
- expected act title.

After G18 the model supplies only:
- `claim`;
- `kind`;
- `act` identity/alias.

The runtime owns:
- alias normalization;
- canonical act identity;
- official act title;
- ELI descriptor;
- official source URL;
- source registry date.

## Important limitation

G18 is deterministic but the initial consolidated-text descriptors are **pinned**.

This intentionally removes hallucinated URLs from the model, but it does not yet prove on every session that the pinned descriptor is still the newest appropriate official text.
