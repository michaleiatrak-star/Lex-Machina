## Goal

A document may be large or partly scanned, but no page may disappear silently because it lacks a text layer or exceeds model context.

## Implemented

- all-pages PDF inspection through `PdfJsDocumentPageSource`;
- page-by-page classification;
- pages with insufficient digital text are routed to local OCR;
- primary OCR adapter: PaddleOCR PP-OCRv6 with `lang=pl`;
- OCR worker renders only requested pages with PyMuPDF;
- every requested OCR page must return an accounted result;
- a page may become BLANK only after the OCR path has processed it and returned no text;
- missing OCR result → fail closed;
- large document output is split into ordered chunks with explicit page markers;
- very long individual pages are split into numbered page-parts;
- chunk validation proves source-character preservation and page accounting;
- default safety envelope: 512 MiB, 10,000 pages, 100,000,000 extracted characters, 24,000 characters/chunk.

## Local OCR installation

The production worker is in `app/ocr/paddle_worker.py` and its dependencies are in `app/ocr/requirements.txt`.

The first model setup may download weights. After the model cache is prepared, OCR inference runs locally.
